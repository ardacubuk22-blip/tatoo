About Us

Home Antique Home is an antiques and collectables house based in İstanbul. The greater part of the collection has been brought from Europe, selected piece by piece: French silver plate, German porcelain, English blue and white plates and examples of period glasswork.

We care about the authenticity and the condition of every piece; most of what we offer are signed or marked works of their period. Our aim is to carry the carefully made objects of the past into present day homes, and to give our customers a purchase they can trust.
